About This Practice Exam

This is an independent study tool built to help candidates prepare for the NSCA Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist (CSCS) exam.

Content Source

Questions are original practice content authored to match the domains, subtopics, and cognitive-level (Recall / Application / Analysis) weightings published in the CSCS Detailed Content Outline (DCO), effective July 1, 2025, and styled after the sample questions included in that outline.

Exam 2 covers the same DCO domains and topics as Exam 1, but is intentionally written at a much higher difficulty: it leans heavily toward Application- and Analysis-level items and uses closely related, tightly-clustered answer options, so surface-level elimination rarely works and only strong domain knowledge reliably identifies the correct answer.
